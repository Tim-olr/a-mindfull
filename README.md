## What is A Mindfull?

A Mindfull is an extraction roguelike shooter where your greatest weapon is your **spirit** — a unique ability tied to who you are as a player. Drop in, loot, fight, and survive long enough to extract with your haul. Each run is shaped by the upgrades you discover along the way.

---

## The Spirit System

The core of A Mindfull revolves around a mechanic called **embracing your spirit** — bringing your spirit into the physical world to activate its abilities.

| Ability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Active Ability** | A powerful, on-demand skill triggered while your spirit is embraced. |
| **Passive Ability** | A constant bonus that applies whenever your spirit is embraced. |

> Embracing your spirit is a high-risk, high-reward decision. Timing and awareness are everything.

---

## How to Play

1. **Drop in** — Enter a run and start exploring. Enemies and loot are waiting.
2. **Embrace your spirit** — Unlock your active and passive abilities to gain an edge.
3. **Find upgrades** — Discover roguelike upgrades throughout the run that amplify your spirit and loadout.
4. **Loot & extract** — Fill your inventory, then fight your way to the extraction point.
5. **Return to the hub** — All extracted loot carries over. Sell it, craft with it, and gear up for the next run.
